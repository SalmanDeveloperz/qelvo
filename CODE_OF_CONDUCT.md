# Code of Conduct

Qelvo is built by people from many places and backgrounds, many of them working on it in their spare time. We want it to stay a place where anyone can show up with a question, a bug report or a half-finished idea and be treated well.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Most friction is a misunderstanding.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone filed a confusing first issue once.
- Accept a "no" on a feature gracefully, and give one kindly.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone's resume or personal details, and never pressure anyone to share theirs.

## What we don't accept

Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, discriminatory jokes, publishing other people's private information, and any conduct that would be out of place in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who does so, in any Qelvo space: the repository, discussions, and our social channels.

To report something, contact a maintainer privately: message [@SalmanDeveloperz](https://github.com/SalmanDeveloperz) on GitHub or [Qelvo on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/qelvo/). Reports are kept confidential.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
